01 / MY STORY

More than just
a student.

Hey! I'm Gaurav Kumar, a 19-year-old figuring things out, one experience at a time.

I completed my schooling up to Class 12 in Patna, Bihar. Those years were an important part of my journey, helping me build the foundation for the person I am becoming today. After finishing school, I took the next step toward my interests in technology and problem-solving.

I'm currently pursuing Computer Science Engineering at Mahindra University. I'm excited to explore the world of computing, understand how technology works behind the scenes, and discover the different possibilities that a career in technology can offer.

Outside academics, I'm someone who enjoys a good anime, an interesting conversation, and learning something new. I like exploring ideas beyond my coursework because I believe that personal growth comes from being curious about more than one thing.

Over the last five to six months, I've also been learning about the stock market and trading. Understanding market movements, price action, risk management, and the psychology behind financial decisions has become an interesting learning experience for me.

I'm still at the beginning of my journey, and that's what makes it exciting. I want to keep learning, challenge my own assumptions, develop useful skills, and make the most of the opportunities that come my way.

This website is a small introduction to who I am right now — a student, a learner, and a work in progress. There's a lot more to discover, and this is only the beginning.
